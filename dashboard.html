Pod Priority Preemption DoS

Reproducing, detecting, and stopping a Denial-of-Service attack that abuses Kubernetes pod priority & preemption in a shared (multi-tenant) cluster.

1How the attack works

😈
Malicious tenantcreates pods in bad-tenant
⬆️
Very high priority+ large CPU requests
⚖️
Scheduler preemptsevicts lower-priority pods to make room
💥
Denial of Servicevictims stuck Pending

Key insight: the damage comes from reserved CPU requests and preemption rights — not from real CPU load. That is why kubectl top can look quiet while the cluster is down.

2Result: before vs after the defenses

3Three layers of defense

Mitigation 1

Resource Quota

Caps bad-tenant at 2 CPU / 10 pods, so large-scale pod stuffing is impossible.

resourcequota.yml
Mitigation 2

Non-preempting class

A legitimate high-priority class that schedules high but cannot evict others (preemptionPolicy: Never).

nonpreempting-priorityclass.yml
Mitigation 3 · primary

Kyverno admission policy

Rejects any attacker pod using a preempting class at creation time, before scheduling.

restrict-priority-class.yml
